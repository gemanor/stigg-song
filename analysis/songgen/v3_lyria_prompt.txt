"I'M AN AI FOUNDER": a satirical electroclash meets hyperpop duet, 128 BPM, A minor, about 2 minutes 20 seconds, very short intro. Two characters. HIM: an oblivious San Francisco AI startup founder; male, mid-twenties, flat deadpan talk-sing, bored, very clear diction. HER: a sharp, sarcastic woman who roasts him; female, bright sassy vocals, sings sweetly-sarcastic and talk-raps her verse with attitude. A gang of voices shouts "FOUNDER!" after every hook line. Crisp 808s, stuttering trap hi-hats, blippy arpeggios, massive detuned supersaw drops on four-on-the-floor kicks with handclaps. Glossy, punchy, funny, TikTok-ready. Spoken lines are spoken, not sung.
