### "See you after tokens"

Searches found no exact match, so this isn't an existing meme. It still lands, because tokens became the scene's currency in 2026:

- Jensen Huang (Mar 2026) said he'd be "deeply alarmed" if a $500K engineer didn't burn at least $250K of tokens ([The Decoder](https://the-decoder.com/nvidia-ceo-jensen-huang-says-hed-be-deeply-alarmed-if-a-500k-developer-spent-less-than-250k-on-ai-tokens)).
- "Tokenmaxxing": Meta's internal "Claudeonomics" leaderboard tracked token use across 85,000+ staff, ranked the top 250 and handed out titles like "Token Legend". It came down in April 2026 ([WBUR](https://www.wbur.org/onpoint/2026/04/28/tokenmaxxing-how-tech-workers-are-gamifying-their-way-to-unemployment), [FourWeekMBA](https://fourweekmba.com/ai-meta-claudeonomics-tokenmaxxing-goodharts-law/)).
- Claude Code limits reset on a rolling 5-hour window plus weekly caps, so "see you when my limit resets" is real life ([Morph](https://www.morphllm.com/claude-code-usage-limits)).

It riffs on "see you after hours" and on post-AGI sign-offs. Read it as: he's only free once his token budget runs dry. The visual pun is arcade tokens. His usage meter hits 100%, he stumbles outside blinking at the sun, the meter resets, and he sprints back in. Text: `INSERT TOKENS TO CONTINUE`.

## 3. Avoid list

1. **Apple Vision Pro** (bg). A headset that never became street wear, and an outsider's shorthand for "tech guy".
2. **Patagonia vest on the founder.** It's the ZIRP-era "Midtown uniform". SF stylists said to drop it ([SF Standard](https://sfstandard.com/2023/12/20/san-francisco-stylists-tech-fashion)), and Sifted says the gilet now signals the *investor*. Only the VC wears one.
3. **Allbirds.** "Tech bros won't even wear this shoe anymore" ([SF Standard](https://sfstandard.com/2023/01/26/tech-bros-are-so-over-sf-they-wont-even-wear-this-shoe-anymore/)).
4. **Black turtleneck** (bg). Reads as Jobs or Theranos.
5. **Ping-pong, beanbags, kombucha taps.** ZIRP perks. "Work hard, play hard is over" (Forbes, above).
6. **Lamborghini, yacht, money gun.** The 2026 flex is quiet: tokens, Waymo, merch, sleep scores.
7. **Crypto: laser eyes, NFTs, "gm".** That was 2021–22. The founder of Celsius the crypto lender got 12 years ([Forklog](https://forklog.com/en/tag/celsius-network)), so keep the can generic.
8. **Metaverse, Google Glass, Humane Pin, Rabbit R1** (bg). Dead gadgets, usable only in a "graveyard" gag.
9. **2010s and Hollywood shorthand:** "disrupt", "Uber for X", unicorn and rocket emoji, HBO *Silicon Valley* quotes, a hooded hacker in Matrix code. Now it's ARR, agents, tokens and cracked.
10. **LinkedIn broetry, "rise and grind".** Outsider hustle content. Insiders post on X and say "996" and "lock in".
11. **"GPU-rich vs GPU-poor".** A 2023 framing. Tokens are the 2026 flex.
12. **AGI House or real people.** A Sept 2026 NYT investigation reported assault allegations there ([GV Wire](https://gvwire.com/2026/09/21/the-ai-party-house-where-networking-has-a-dark-side/)). No real likenesses or logos.

## 4. Character: the founder

- **Age:** 24. The average YC founder is now about 26 ([Rebel Fund](https://jaredheyman.medium.com/on-the-new-y-combinator-3c28e548896c)).
- **Silhouette:** "Jacked, cracked." Broad shoulders under an oversized hoodie, thin legs, huge shoes, head jutting forward from tech neck. The outline is a top-heavy triangle on stilts with boats.
- **Hair:** Faded sides and a messy curly top spilling out under the cap. When the cap comes off for "founder mode", the hair springs up.
- **Face:**
  - Wide, unblinking eyes with blue screen-rectangle reflections, and 996 eye bags.
  - A permanent half-smirk, and a pouch bulge under the upper lip that switches sides.
  - One white earbud.
  - For one gag, chunky AI glasses with camera dots and a red recording light.
- **Outfit:**
  - A faded, unstructured merch dad cap reading "building".
  - An oversized heather-grey merch hoodie with a made-up startup name and a meaningless glyph. For investor scenes, a navy merch half-zip.
  - Tapered charcoal joggers.
  - Chunky white runners with an exaggerated "pod" sole.
  - White crew socks pulled high.
- **Accessories:**
  - A screenless fabric band. TechCrunch calls Whoop the "unofficial uniform" of gym bros ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/10/13/oura-is-winning-young-women-and-losing-gym-rats-and-its-fine-with-that)).
  - A matte smart ring. Wearing both lets him compare sleep scores.
  - A Tech Week lanyard with stacked badges.
  - The faded tin circle on his back pocket.
- **Props (generic):**
  - The cans, the iced matcha and the puck tin.
  - A phone showing a vertical green line.
  - The token plaque and the silver agent box on a leash.
  - A rolled sleeping bag.
- **Posture:** Two modes.
  - "Locked in": hunched, hood up, thumbs flying, face lit blue.
  - "Founder mode": wide stance, chin up, laptop held open one-handed like a waiter's tray, slow-motion walk to camera.
- **Attitude:** Deadpan, earnest and under-slept. He says "insane" flat, never laughs, never drinks, and never touches cash; money is only charts and tokens.
- **Palette:** Greys, with matcha green as the one accent, which doubles as "the green chart".
