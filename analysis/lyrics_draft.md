## Why the new lines (sources in research_culture.md)

- **Pre-chorus A:**
  - X bios flipping to "building" or "Founder at Stealth" is a tracked genre.
  - "Annualized a Tuesday" is the 2025-26 "vibe revenue" / ARR-inflation story (Fortune, Sept 2025; Cluely's retracted revenue number).
  - "Agents for your agents" is literal since lead agents manage teammate agents (Feb 2026).
- **Pre-chorus B:**
  - It covers $20 "unlimited" plans, power users who cost more than they pay, and inference eating gross margin.
  - It sets up "See you after tokens" and lands the Stigg end card without naming Stigg.
- **"Hold my Celsius"** works because the 2026 founder doesn't drink. "Sober grind" is the insider trend.
