# I'M AN AI FOUNDER: lyrics v3, for director review

An original song inspired by the director's draft (not an edit of it).
It's a duet with spoken dialogue:
- **HIM** is the founder: oblivious, deadpan, earnest.
- **HER** roasts him and translates his founder-speak into plain English. In the end she turns out to be the real founder.

Status: **lyrics approved. Master v4 (`audio/im_an_ai_founder_v4.mp3`, intro C, new closing scene) is waiting for the director's sign-off. No video spend until then.**

Delivery:
- HIM: flat, bored talk-sing, like a guy reading his own X bio.
- HER: bright and sharp, with sing-song sarcasm. Her chorus answers use a playground-taunt melody.
- The chant: one note per syllable, stadium call-and-response.

```
[SKIT 1 — spoken, muffled party behind a filtered beat]
HER:  Hey. What are you doing tonight?
HIM:  Hold my Celsius.
HIM:  I can't go on a date. I'm an AI founder.

[DROP — gang chant]

I'M AN AI FOUNDER! (FOUNDER!)

He couldn't feel his fingers, but he wired me the cash.
Matcha's at a record high, there's a shortage in Japan.
```
