AI founders are all around us, and they deserve their own MTV gag. See y'all after tokens.

See you After Tokens, Nov 10 in San Francisco: https://luma.com/after-tokens

"See you after tokens" is what an AI founder says instead of yes. He doesn't take a salary, he takes a token budget. His co-founder is a Mac mini, his seed closed in a cold plunge, and his valuation is marked to the price of matcha. He can't come out tonight. Maybe after Series B.

Every word he sings burns tokens off a live meter, and every word she sings is free. He runs out on the exact frame the music stops. She pays, and the music comes back on her dime. It's something to dance to while your agents run up the bill, and a receipt for everyone who has ever annualized a Tuesday.
