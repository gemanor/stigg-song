# PLAN: "I'M AN AI FOUNDER" × Stigg (one page)

**Concept.** A deadpan SF AI founder sings his own pitch deck while his date live-corrects it in red pen. The whole video runs on his **token meter**: every word he sings burns tokens, until the song itself runs out of tokens on the word "tokens". The music dies, she pays, and the music comes back. **MAKE EVERY TOKEN COUNT.** Then the Stigg logo (first and only appearance).

**Spine.** A Stigg-style usage meter in the HUD:

- 1,000,000 → burns per sung word → "quarter-million" −250k → LOW BALANCE in the final chorus → **0 on the music cut (2:24)** → USAGE BLOCKED.
- Her phone tap: TOP-UP APPROVED → the end card.

**Look.** Stigg's own palette and its After Tokens banner language:

- #131512 ground, #92E759 green, #FF776D coral, off-white;
- pixel-grid green type, corner crosshairs, hairline rules.

Five looks cut on downbeats: AFTER TOKENS (dark), PITCH DECK (light), SIGNAL GREEN (flat slams), DICTIONARY (editorial), TERMINAL (UI). His type is glossy green hype; hers is coral serif and red-pen marks. Every "SEE YOU AFTER TOKENS" is the banner lockup.
